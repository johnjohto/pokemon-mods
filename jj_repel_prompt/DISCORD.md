**Repel Reuse Prompt**

When a Repel expires, the game asks **Use another?** Choose **YES** to spend
the weakest Repel in your Bag, or **NO** to keep the classic behavior. It does
not use items from PC storage.

`jj_repel_prompt` v1.0.0

Download: https://github.com/johnjohto/pokemon-mods/releases/tag/jj_repel_prompt-v1.0.0

Keep the `.zip` intact. Drag it onto the start screen or use **Import mod .zip**
on the **MODS** tab. The [full guide](README.md) covers prerequisites,
troubleshooting, and contributor tests.
