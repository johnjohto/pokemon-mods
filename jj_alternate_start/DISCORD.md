**Alternate Start**

Begin a new game in Viridian, Pewter, Cerulean, Lavender, Vermilion, Celadon,
Saffron, or Indigo Plateau. **PALLET (CLASSIC)** remains vanilla. With
**STORY BEATS** on, Oak and Blue meet you at the new Pokémon Center. With it
off, you receive the starter during Oak's speech and take the quick start.

`jj_alternate_start` v2.0.1

Download: https://github.com/johnjohto/pokemon-mods/releases/tag/jj_alternate_start-v2.0.1

Keep the `.zip` intact. Drag it onto the start screen or use **Import mod .zip**
on the **MODS** tab. Enable it before starting a new game. Read [README.md](README.md)
before choosing a starting save.
