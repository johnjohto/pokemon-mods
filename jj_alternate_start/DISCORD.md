**Alternate Start**

Why start in Pallet Town every single time? Begin your journey in any of the eleven towns and cities, with the tutorial already behind you.

:star2: Pick Bulbasaur, Charmander, or Squirtle at the end of Oak's speech
:house: Pick your hometown and spawn at its Pokémon Center (blackouts and escape ropes bring you back there too)
:clipboard: Pokédex in hand, parcel quest done, Viridian old man off the road
:video_game: Pick **PALLET (CLASSIC)** and the intro stays 100% vanilla

**New in 1.1 — Story Beats:** Blue doesn't disappear from your journey. His ambushes are re-gated on gym badges, so they happen in order even without the Pallet tutorial: Route 22 after badge 1, Cerulean after 2, S.S. Anne after 3, Pokémon Tower after 4, Silph Co. after 6. Toggleable in the mod's options, and it only touches saves started with the mod.

Only affects new games started while the mod is on.
