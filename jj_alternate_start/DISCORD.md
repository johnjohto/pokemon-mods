**Alternate Start**

Why start in Pallet Town every single time? Begin your journey in any of nine towns and cities — with the story intact.

:house: Pick your hometown at the end of Oak's speech and spawn at its Pokémon Center
:older_man: **Oak and Blue come to see you off!** Pick your starter from Oak's balls in person
:crossed_swords: Blue takes the counter-pick and duels you on the spot, just like the lab
:bookmark_tabs: Oak hands you the Pokédex himself, and Blue keeps showing up all game: his ambushes are re-gated on badges (Route 22 after 1, Cerulean after 2, S.S. Anne after 3, Tower after 4, Silph after 6)
:video_game: Prefer the quiet quick start? Toggle **STORY BEATS** off in the mod options. PALLET (CLASSIC) stays 100% vanilla

Every town has a guaranteed way out: Fuchsia and Cinnabar would softlock a fresh save so they're not offered, and a Saffron start opens the thirsty guards' gates.
