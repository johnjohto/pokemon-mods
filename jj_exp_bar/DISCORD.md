**EXP Bar**

A Gen 2-style bar under the active Pokémon's HP bar shows progress toward its
next level. It animates on experience gain, wraps through level-ups, and tracks
only the active battler. Choose INK, GEN 2 BLUE, or HP MATCH, plus BORDER or
GEN 2 position.

`jj_exp_bar` v1.1.1

Download: https://github.com/johnjohto/pokemon-mods/releases/tag/jj_exp_bar-v1.1.1

Keep the `.zip` intact. Drag it onto the start screen or use **Import mod .zip**
on the **MODS** tab. See [README.md](README.md) for widescreen and 3D-BTL use.
