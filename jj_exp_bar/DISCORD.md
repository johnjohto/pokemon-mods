**EXP Bar**

Gen 1 never showed you how close you were to a level-up. Now a Gen 2-style experience bar sits under your HP bar in battle and fills as you earn EXP — fanfare wrap and all.

:chart_with_upwards_trend: Fills live as your active Pokémon gains EXP, wrapping through level-ups
:art: Three fill styles in the options menu: classic ink, Gen 2 blue, or HP-match (green/yellow/red)
:sleeping: EXP.ALL shares and switch-ins behave just like Gold/Silver

No setup: install and forget it.
