**Running Shoes**

Hold **B** to run on foot at up to 2X speed. Configure **RUN BUTTON** as HOLD,
TOGGLE, or ALWAYS, then choose independent bicycle and Surf speeds. Encounters,
poison, daycare experience, and Repel still count completed tiles.

`jj_running_shoes` v1.1.1

Download: https://github.com/johnjohto/pokemon-mods/releases/tag/jj_running_shoes-v1.1.1

Keep the `.zip` intact. Drag it onto the start screen or use **Import mod .zip**
on the **MODS** tab. See [README.md](README.md) for every option.
