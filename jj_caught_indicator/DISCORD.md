**Caught Indicator**

Ever weaken a wild pokémon, then check the dex to see if you even need it? No more. If you already caught one, a little pokeball icon shows up right next to its name in battle, just like the later generations.

:white_check_mark: Icon next to the name = already in your Pokédex
:x: No icon = new catch, time to get to work
:art: A **CAUGHT ICON** option with two marks: **GEN 2** (default, filled with a glint) or **GEN 1** (a small open ball)
:new: **New in 1.2.0** — full support for the **widescreen battle layout** added in gen1recomp v0.1.31; the mark follows the foe's HP bar instead of landing on its name
:vibration_mode: **New in 1.3.0** — an **ICON SHAKE** toggle: the mark shakes along with the foe's name by default, or switch it off to keep it still and easier to read
:bulb: Also in 1.3.0 — the mark is now dimmed by the battle's screen flash like everything else, instead of sitting crisp on top of it

Wild battles only, purely visual, nothing else changes. Install and forget it.
