**Caught Indicator**

Wild battles show a Poké Ball mark beside a species already registered in your
Pokédex. Choose **GEN 1** or **GEN 2**, enable **LAST BALL** for capture colors,
and decide whether the mark follows the classic HUD shake. Trainer and link
battles are unchanged.

`jj_caught_indicator` v1.4.0

Download: https://github.com/johnjohto/pokemon-mods/releases/tag/jj_caught_indicator-v1.4.0

Keep the `.zip` intact. Drag it onto the start screen or use **Import mod .zip**
on the **MODS** tab. See [README.md](README.md) for layout and save details.
